How to Report a Team Killer by funkystudios

funkystudios

Posted: 10 months ago
Edited: 10 months ago
When reporting players for team killing, follow these rules.
  • You must create a new topic and include some form of evidence (pictures or video). The more evidence, the better! 
  • Simply giving a claim is not enough for us to deal with a player, unless we have many similar reports on the player. 
  • Make sure to include the player's username that you are reporting!
