Hi guys! This is my map Simba. First off, there are 4 monuments that are not to far from your spawn. Unfortunately, I would personally expect people to mishaps use this sniper to much witch would be a pretty long game, so I figured to shorten all of it to 5 shots only. about 50 blocks away from the other teams base, so good luck building a bridge!
