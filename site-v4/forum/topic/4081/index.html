On nebula there are a variety of Gamemodes CTM TDM CTF and soon to be more with the many ideas pooling in from the forums I hope one of those new gamemodes could be my idea Zone Control. Zone Control is a mode where there are three "zones" the map would look like this
                                                                   Red Spawn
                                         Left Zone           Center Zone          Right Zone                   
                                                                    Blue spawn

1. How to capture a zone, At the Center of each zone there is a STONE pressure plate step on it (for the mapmaker's desired amount of time) and the zone is yours      ( comment below on how you think the zone's control should be displayed )
2. How to earn points, After you gain control of the zone Every 5 seconds you get 1 point.If all zones are in your control you get 5 points (+2 bonus points)
3. Taking back a zone, If the opposing team has a zone and you want it back step on the pressure plate (again, for the mapmakers set amount of time) and it is yours
4. End of game, There are two ways this mode could end 1. A set score limit 2. A time limit like TDM
Optional additions
1. Make it so that once a zone is captured the team can't camp in it
2. My idea is that when a zone is captured fireworks of the capturing team's color go off
3. If all zones are captured by one team they should get better items stronger materials, armor, Gold apples to God apples, Etc.
