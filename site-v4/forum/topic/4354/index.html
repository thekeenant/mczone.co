Ok, this ain't a suggestion. This will never be added into minecraft itself, let alone MC Zone. Its just an idea I came up with which would help. A lot. I'm only collecting opinions.

The idea:
You know how in Call of Duty you get KillCam, Final Kill, that kind of thing. It records someone elses gameplay for you to watch on your screen, right? Well what if there was something like that in Minecraft?
This could be an option when someone is spectating someone else. There could be a command for it like /pers xXePicDeMoNSlAyEr1337Xx <----- example name :P which will show you their perspective of the entire match they just played in. Better than watching them walking round following their compass, right?

How would it be useful?
1. Answers questions
Normally when you kill someone, they like to ask questions. Examples: "How many hearts did you have?" "How did you sneak up behind me like that?" "WTH???? HOW DID YOU DO THAT?? I HAD FULL IRON AND DIAMOND SWORD!!! HOW THE *!$#@ DID YOU KILL ME???" Well, now your questions are easily answered by watching their perspective! You could learn a few tricks as well as the PVP techniques they used to kill you. Pretty useful.
2. Were they hacking?
Think you got killed by a hacker? Want to make sure before you report it? Watch what they did to kill you, then record it and send it to us, for all the evidence we need that someone is hacking! It will make everything so much easier when it comes to reporting... makes our job a lot easier too...
3. Brightens up YouTube videos
If you were a youtuber and were teaming with someone without recording software, why not watch their perspective afterwards, record it and edit in some of their clips to your video, so people can see how well both of you did? Especially if this teammate got an amazing kill and you weren't there to see it, and they weren't able to record it... think of Bluexephos' videos as an example; it shows Lewis and Simon's perspective in the same video every time something interesting happens, and it makes things much better than a singles players perspective.. it doesn't just have to be your teammates either... could be the person who killed you, whatever...

Why wont this be added?
Unfortunately, CoD has its own recording software for this kind of thing. Minecraft doesn't. The owners of this non-existent plugin would have to make some kind of partnership with Fraps or some other recording software company, and that would be expensive, especially for a company/person which only makes plugins... also, recording up to 50 peoples gameplay at the same time... that's bound to create lag...

But you gotta admit it would be pretty cool.

Post your opinions down below :D
