Hi all,

I've made a new map designed specifically for Nebula. It is my first Nebula map, however I have made adventure maps before as part of TheIvyCraft. The map is called Checkmate, as it is themed on the board game Chess. It is a CTM map with two monuments to destroy per team (They are in the Rooks). I have tested it to look good in Dokucraft (High, Dark, Light), John Smith and of course Default. I designed it as a regular player of Nebula to suit the game from the ground up. If you like the map please show your support and hopefully the admins will pick it.

Update: Changed Items in chests to stop Team Killers and changed name!

Spec
- Two Teams (Black and White)
- Teams spawn in their coloured king
- You play as the Pawns
- Centre Zone (Chessboard)
- Large swords hanging in the sky (Why Not?)
- Massive Chess pieces - Kings, Queens (Captured), Bishops, Knights, Rooks
- Two monuments per team in the Rooks
- Iron in the King's heads
- Loot in the queen's head, only accessible by the team of the same colour as the Queen
- Great potential for Bridge Fighting, and open fighting
- The Bows in the Kits have Infinity
- All the armour in the Loot Chests have Protection 4
- The bows in the Loot Chests have Infinity 1, Punch 1, Power 1 and Flame 1
- The swords in the Loot Chests have Sharpness 3
