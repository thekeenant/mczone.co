I'm so disappointed in this last game I played in walls.  I like to play walls more than the other games because of the teamwork involved.  Maybe it's an "implied" teamwork, but when I'm on my team, let's say yellow, I help all the yellows out.

If people need stuff like coal, string, arrows, iron, or even diamonds, I'll give it to them freely.  Since we're a team, we're trying to get all yellows op, right? Am I wrong?

Well, this last game I played, I had a nice base down in the ravine in the jungle map.  I had 4 furnaces, full iron with sword, a chest full of goodies, and a door.  I invited my teammates to come join me.  It was a welcoming environment.

So, two yellows storm in, take all the extra iron in my furnaces, without saying a word.  Then one keeps mining the blocks below me.  I run out of my base to escape.  Then they blocked my wooden door entrance with cobble and took over my base!!

I told them that they were being "unfriendly" especially after I shared my stuff.  So I decided to go up to the surface and give up my base.  Then, they (the 2 bullies) follow me up and use splash potions of weakness on me (from my goodie chest) and one tries to fire charge me, but I ran away.

Next, I declare again in chat that they are trying to kill me and another yellow in a skybase, my other teammate, calls me rude names.  My final message was "This is walls, not HG or SG" before someone killed me.

So I had team killers and chat abusers in the same game on my yellow team.  It was awful and I felt disappointed that some people don't view the game as I do.  As a TEAM experience.

Thanks for letting me vent.
