Game Mode - Chunks by ViolentBoo

ViolentBoo

Posted: 2 months ago
Edited: 2 months ago
   Back Story: I was in a middle of an HG match and I got all my equipment that I needed for a good starting battle when the grace period ends. So I have a stone sword and my pickax and some food. That's all I need. So the game starts and I kill around 3-5 people. Nothing special then I just start to mine. Then I felt something. I don't usually think of it at all.

   Mercy.

   I kind of felt bad for the people that I kill and I wanted for them to have a chance to fight me to see what they're made of. But most the time they're just in their crafting table doing nothing. So here's what's up.

   Gameplay: So in this game-mode you spawn in one chunk (or maybe a 2x2 which ever funky prefers) In that chunk you have fifteen minutes to prepare for a battle with eight other players. In that fifteen minutes you can find food, mine, anything you want for the fifteen minutes before the battle. When the time is up, you're spawned into an arena with seven other players and will must fight to the death. Who ever is standing alive will go back to their chunk for an extra ten minutes to prepare for the last battle (or next if their is a lot of people). So when that's over you'll end up versing harder people because they've won their own death match. This will continue until there is one player left.


  Starting Equipment: With every kit (if funky decides to make this and make kits) you start off with 5 logs and 1 Raw beef. I choose these items because you never know if there is no trees or animals or any source of food to your disposal. And I think that the kits should be like the ones from HG. If not similar.

What do you think?
