Project Nebula & Server Changes by funkystudios

funkystudios

Posted: 7 months ago
Edited: 7 months ago
In less than two weeks we will be rolling out some big changes to MC Zone that will impact everyone on the server. The increased player count has lead us to make improvements to the lobby and voting as well as a new server game mode.

Project Nebula (Merge) Coming 4/19 - 4/20
Nexus MC to the next level; Fight against 2-4 teams in various game modes such as team deathmatch, capture the monument and more. Fight in multiple themed maps and earn points for your team to win the game!

Equipped with its own project developer, Haribo98 (also the creator), this server will be constantly modified to improve gameplay. The merge has also brought 2 new administrators and 2 moderators as noted on the staff page. 

New Lobby Coming 4/19 - 4/20
As many of you know, the MC Zone lobby needs changes. Our latest competition for a new hub produced great results, but nothing the size of what we need (many didn't fit requirements). We have started on a new hub that will include various mini-games for  while you are waiting, and a nicely themed environment to enjoy with other players.

Credits & New Voting System Coming 4/25 - 4/28
Earn credits by the kill and by the win. Spend credits on temporary kit uses as well as in game items and enchantments. Soon we will implement a voting page for MC Zone in general. Voting will get you x amount of credits, enough for a temporary kit.

Most of these ideas are final and are not subject to change. Reply to this topic with your thoughts about the above changes!
