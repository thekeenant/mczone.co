MC Zone Rules

1. General Rules

  1. Inappropriate or aggressive language should not be used (casual atmosphere).
  2. Do not spam.
  3. Do not advertise servers that are not a part of MC Zone (instant ban).
  4. Follow directions from the MC Zone staff.
  5. Use common sense.
  6. Be respectful.
  7. No team griefing or team killing.

2. Client Modifications

  1. Any client modifications added that give you an advantage over other Minecraft players is not allowed.
  2. The following mods are OK to use:
    • Optifine
    • Toggle-crouch
  3. The following mods are strictly prohibited from gameplay:
    • Macro Commands
    • Inventory Sorters
    • Auto-sprint
    • Automatic tool selectors
    • Minimaps (REI's is OK)
    • X-ray of any kind
    • PVP related modifications
    • Fly mods of any kind
  4. For any other mods that you are unsure of, ask a mod for help. When in doubt, remove it.

3. Forum Rules

  1. No spamming topics.
  2. Post topics in the correct categories.
  3. Do not recreate recently locked topics.
  4. No offensive texts, images, or links.
  5. Casual atmosphere on the forums - no aggressive language.
  6. Reloading topics to gain views is a no-no.
  7. Post signatures are unnecessary!
