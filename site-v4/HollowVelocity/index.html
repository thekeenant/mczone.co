Although I experienced the game prior to actually purchasing the full copy, I formally started around the middle of December 2012. Two of my friends showed me around multiplayer servers and how they work. I was soon showed MC Zone and played a game of 'The Walls' for the first time, I unexpectedly did well!

I applied on April 26 2013 and was accepted nearly three months later on July 21 2013. A quote for future applicants...
