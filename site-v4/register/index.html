Register Create a MC Zone account

Although we keep track of all our users, even those who haven't created an MC Zone account, creating an account allows for you to post on our forums and engage more with our community. Registering for Minecraft Zone is easy and quick! Simply login to one of our servers and type the command, "/register [password]", where [password] is the password you will use when logging into the website. Once you have created a password, you can click the account button on the top right and login using your Minecraft username and MC Zone password.


If you would like to change your current password, use "/register [old password] [new password]". If you do not remember your current password, please contact us at info@mczone.co.
