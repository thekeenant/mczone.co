How to use:


Start out by making a password. Type in "/wa password YOURPASSWORD". Your username is your minecraft username.


Players can then go to the shop in Terrascape and deposit items, when they do this the items disappear from the world and get logged into the players online account. Log on to the shop website using your username and the password that you set up earlier. There you can see current auctions going on.

Using your Terrascape dollars (TSD) they can buy items, and they will be sent to the mailbox in game (a sign) and they can set up their own auctions for the items they have stored online. Right click the Deposit sign with items you want to sell to place them in the online account. Right click the withdraw sign to retrieve items you have purchased or cancele

One of the main features is that the website will look at all the sales and calculate a moving average for the market price of that item. Which should help players when setting a price, or buyers to see if they are getting a good deal.
