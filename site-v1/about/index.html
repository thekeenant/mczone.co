About

What is MC Zone?

MC Zone hosts many gaming Minecraft servers, each offers a different community of people and style of play. As our community grows, we plan to add more and more servers and games to ensure you are having the most exciting gaming experience possible within the bounds of Minecraft. Head to the homepage to view our current games.

The Story Behind MC Zone

MC Zone was founded, created, coded, currently maintained and administered by me, funkystudios. I started my first server back in August of 2011. This server was founded by the name of "TerraScape" and became increasingly popular but experienced a decline in popularity as more servers arose and created greater competition.

I was left with a friendly community of around 75 reliable players. To support TerraScape's monthly server costs I strongly encouraged donations and eventually created a "Hunger Games" server that was very popular at the time. I ended up creating four hunger games servers that were often filled with players. Again, my servers experienced another decline in popularity. More hunger games servers popped up offering different and new ways to play the game.

During the summer of 2012 I developed a plugin that would run the popular minigame, "The Walls". Starting with one walls server, I eventually added two more as this game became the most popular out of the three that I had started.

As you can see, there is a pattern to my servers. I create the server, people love it, and after a couple months that server or game becomes less popular causing me to start a new server. People literally forget about the old games that they used to love. I realized what I was missing - a link between all of the gaming servers I have developed, so I made MC Zone. Now you can simply go to mczone.co choose a MC game of your liking and join the server! Here you can also find player statistics and leaderboards. Hope you enjoy playing on MC Zone!

Frequently Asked Questions

Below are questions often asked by users at MC Zone. If you do not find an answer to your question here, please visit the contact page and ask a question directly to an admin or me.


I don't have a PayPal account, what do I do?
If you are looking to purchase a status upgrade, the following is not possible. However, if you are buying kits, it is possible to enter a credit card or debit card rather than a PayPal account. We do not accept hard cash payments, just online via PayPal.

What does 'recurring' mean when buying VIP, Elite, or Titan?
This means that you will make a payment once per month at the price specified. PayPal will automatically charge you in order to keep your upgraded status of VIP, Elite, or Titan.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Simply head to the order page, type in your username, and click "Cancel Renewal". This will stop future payments and continue your subscription until the end of the month you paid for.
