My opinion on the Avicus update is that it is very complex, filled with good and bad changes. Personally, I would have wished that we (the staff that weren't included) would have a say in this drastic new chapter for MC Zone. But more importantly, I want the players to be happy and stay here in the community.

I have seen that many of the players want to keep Nexus, Hunger Games, Skywars and Survival Games. I know some of these games have been unpopular due to bugs and issues, but we should not just take them away. We should fix the bugs to what the games used to be. I currently want them to stay, these are my reasons and my suggestions for the games:

Nexus, our original Team Death-match mini-game, has been reported to be taken out. Let me tell you this: many of the staff are not happy with this. Personally, that is my favorite game. I know that many others  like it also, so I am here to stand up for the game. I think that it should stay. Some players have stated that it is "boring" and "repetitive", but this is not due to the kit pvp, but to the fact that we have had the same four or five maps forever. I have been pushing for new maps to be added in our staff chat, but I have been told that it isn't happening. I wasn't given the time of day to give my total reasoning. I said it, and got the same responses, "boring and repetitive." How about we add some new kits to spice it up. I know for a fact, the last update was the orc update. Prior to that, only one kit was added. I believe if we added like 4 more kits, it would be changed. Especially if we added free kits to give the players what they want.

Hunger Games, the original game, is now being removed? Its booming with activity. No one, I say no one, can say a good reason for it being taken out. It helps MC Zone out, its currently being used, and players like it! Thats what really matters in the end (for me at least). Don't get me wrong, I love the idea of Survival Games and Hunger Games being combined into the Avicus Games, but there is no need for Hunger Games to be taken out. There are many reasons for Survival Games to be taken out (mainly because there is no production for MC Zone), but give Hunger Games a shot. It is massively popular, and can become even more popular.

Skywars, our original TDM game, the third added, I think should stay. But I know its not popular, not many players on, etc. Do you wish to know the reason for this, its because of 2.0 and the fact that there was a long lasting bug that made it so players can't pick up blocks. I say we add another map, 3.0 maybe, and make it smaller, close quarters, fast-paced like it used to be. This would increase its popularity again, the games are long now, but the old matches were 5-10 minutes. The perfect time for a mini-game! Also, maybe we should advertise the fact that it has been fixed to the players. Maybe through our multi-media (FaceBook and Twitter). Ever since Skywars 2.0 was released, it has been disliked by the players. I agree with all the players that wished for 1.0 to be added back. I was always told to "let it get popular" and "give it some time", I now realize that I should have spoken out for what I thought was right. I take responsability for it to have gone down in players, but I wish for everyone to start playing it. I believe it can be revived.

Ghost Squadron, the newest of the mini-games, has had mmany bugs. Actually, these bugs were never fixed. I pestered for the longest periods of time about the kit spawner bug (couldn't use spawn eggs), but never got answered. This new, revolutionary game would be much more popular if we told the players before a match, what they were actually playing. Many players were confused, which could be easily fixed with a spawn room. Personally, I liked the Beta version the most. The small teams, the original way, made much more fun by adding action with few players compared to these 10v10 games. I liked the idea of having many different games going on at once more than just having one big one. A few more tweaks could be to let command blocks due their work. On some of the old, original maps, command blocks were turned off. This made it so players could not experience the wholeness of the maps.

The gem shop sounds like a great idea! I support it 100%. But maybe keep the credits idea. I don't like the fact that all servers use gems, and now we are! We should revolutionize, make a new name for currency.

This is the end of my post. Those are all the criticism I have. They are mostly directed to the staff to keep these, but also to the players to stand up for what they like, for what MC Zone stands for. After all, "our top priorities ensuring reliability/ease of use, encouraging a clean, positive and fun environment and last but not least, maintaining a high level of quality of our games and servers (avicus.net)," I believe the only way to keep this mentality is for us not to take out games, but improve upon them. If you would wish to support me, skype the staff members that support the taking out of the game. Don't spam the forums with nonsense, don't stop playing the games. Just ask them why, make them give real reasons. Not just "its not popular", "its boring". Ask them to change it, make us, the staff, stand up for ourselves. I might get some negative criticisms for this, but add me on skype: gilletteracer for more information on my opinions.

Thank you for reading,
GilletteRacer74
