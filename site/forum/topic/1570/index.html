So, many people have been asking, when we're going to run the tournament, how to register and the like.
So, after a little bit of planning, we're going to release dates. This means that, we might not be at 500,000 users when the tournament starts. But, that's not such a big issue. Registration will be hosted on the new website, it will look very similar, but have several backend improvements. Here are the official dates for the tournament!

Registration Opens: July 9th 
Registration Closes: August 9th
Tournament Starts: August 16th (Around 8:00PM GMT)

Remember to have a good communication group, such as skype, so your team can easily communicate and join the correct server. Good luck :)

Have fun, and good luck! Make sure you have your teams ready, the tournament is estimated to go on for ~3 hours. So make sure you have time, or make sure your team has enough substitutes so that if one of your team members drops out, you have a replacement. But remember, once the match has started, you can't sub in/out players.
