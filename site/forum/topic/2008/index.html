Hello guys, I have a pretty cool idea for a minigame to be added on MC Zone!! It is called Zombie Survival [ZS]. So, what is it???

How to play:
  • So, when enough players are in a server, a countdown starts and a random player gets selected to be the Zombie.. 
  • The Humans have a 1 minute period to run, built a shelter or something and hide (In some maps they can break blocks , in some others though no). Then when the Zombie finds a player has to hit him twice with a rotten flesh to make him a zombie (All Zombies wear Zombie Heads). So.. The zombies have to get all the humans to be Zombies so they win, but if 10 minutes pass and they haven't made all the Humans to Zombies, Humans win..

  • Kits: Humans can have kits to defend themselves and can kill zombies, but the zombies respawn..
  • Zombie Kits: Zombies can have kits that give them effects (eg. Speed I)
NOTE: If a Zombie catches a Human, it counts 5 credits.. If the Humans escape, it gives each player 10 credits and the same if Zombies win
I hope you like my idea and add it to MC Zone.. If you accept it i'll work on an arena for this game.
