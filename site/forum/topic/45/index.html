Well it appears our new server is not providing enough power to hold the massive influx of players on The Hunger Games, Sky Wars, and Volmaria. The network appears to have a poor upload speed, though the download speed is great. We are moving to a new host which will provide us with a second Dual E5-2620, 64GB RAM.

I'm posting this because many have complained about the latency issues on our servers, especially on The Hunger Games... This upgrade will take care of it!

UPDATE:  Definitely switching hosts. After an unexpected shutdown (hardware failure?), all of our servers were down for an hour. We are planning on switching within a few days.

UPDATE 2: I have ordered the dedicated server, it should arrive within a day :)

UPDATE 3: Server arrived today, starting to migrate servers over. Planning on switch by Thursday.

UPDATE 4: Games have transferred over, mostly. Some bugs exist though it appears to be fairly smooth as of 6:00PM.

UPDATE 5: Now with the release of Nexus coming up we are starting move.... again.... Rather than two extremely powerful CPUs, we are moving to four powerful CPUs, same RAM. This will deal with most of the lag experienced on our servers, especially Nexus.
