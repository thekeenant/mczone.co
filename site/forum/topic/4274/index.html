I have a mixture of original and not-so-original, so hope that's ok.

Original, I think ----

Baseball - have redstone activate the bases as you run around like the plates in Ghost Squadron.  Shoot arrows at targets places around the outfield which tell you where to run to 1B, 2B, 3B, HR.  I guess players could "catch" the arrows by being hit and block the arrow from hitting targets.  Teamed, of course. 

Note Block Showdown - provide endless redstone, repeaters, torches, etc... and pose a challenge song like "Happy Birthday" or "Jingle Bells" that team need to create within 15 minutes. I guess teams would get points for completion - teamed

Not original -----

1 V 1 PVP - Mini course with web and lava traps.  Chest in middle with good sword.  Spawn with arrows only.  I've seen this on youtube, but not on a major server.  Would have a spectator view above the arena. not teamed.

Lava Musical Chairs - Small area surrounded by lava. Platforms rise and lower every like 15 seconds, eventually leaving one player left.  I've played this on another smaller server, and it's very fun.  It involves good parkour skills.  not teamed, individual

Ice Sumo - Push and PVP players off the ice into lava.  Again, I've played this on another smaller server, and it's very fun.
