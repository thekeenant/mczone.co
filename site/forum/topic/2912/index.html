WaveConquerer

Posted: 4 months ago
Edited: 4 months ago
Candoria is one of the oldest maps and I was around when it was released. it was my second fave map (first was lights out).  I still love it, but recently i have been disapointed by it.  Back in the old days, most of the map was used and balanced. If i spawned on the bridge i would fight valiantly and when i was on the lower levels i would become an archer and snipe from the trees by the trees.  Or i would become a barbarian and run around the map wreaking havoc.

Nowadays, all fighting is restricted to the bridge, due to only one team spawn.  There are little to no sniping opportunities.  It is decided on how many people in your group are fending the other.  If you happen to fall to the lower levels, fights are 1v1 rounds scattered on the floor.  It is a waste of land.  If the old multiple spawns came back, they could make all maps better, not just candoria.
