As you may have noticed, there has been a few changes to Nebula recently, first on Alpha, and now on all the other servers. Nebula has been upgraded, to a newer, better version. V2. It gives us a whole new range of features, which will be beneficial both in and out of game. Along with a few other things, which help us with one of our events coming soon.

So, V2 is packed with a few features that we'd like to list;
  • Much better, more flexible API.
  • Ability to set team names
  • Easier to create new game modes
  • Lag removed (:O ikr, still a little bit left...)
  • Ability to set the next map, when testing new maps
So, the other announcement, is the event that will want to host at 500,000 users. A great achievement for MC Zone, and we want to celebrate that with a public tournament! You can register, and manage your own team, of 8 players and 4 Substitutes (recommended, but not required).

A team will require 8 players, and teams with less than 8 will not be entered into the tournament. However, if for whatever reason, a team mate drops out before the match, a substitute will take place, or a team will run with less than 8 players, so I suggest you get some subs!

I'll be releasing more info on this later. Watch this thread!

What's your opinion?
