This is the first nebula map i created so expect to see better maps in the future.
i really love playing nebula everyday on this amazing server so i made this map: Galactic
i made most of the map with my hand but i used MCEdit a little bit. I hope you like it!
What the map has to offer:
  • A REALLY big map
  • A passageway in the ship exposed, leading you to other hidden areas
  • a lot of iron and diamond blocks
  • Spaceships of course! full of iron and a bit of diamond too
  • big map = many places to attack from
