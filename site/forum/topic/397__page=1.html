My HG kit ideas :) by izjust4fun

izjust4fun

Posted: 8 months ago
Edited: 7 months ago

Here are some of my kit ideas :)


Kit name: Leech
Kit info: Instantly after killing a player results in your entire health bar restored (not hunger bar).
Starting items: Compass
Extra info: Great for taking out teams.

Kit name: Endermage
Kit info: Teleport a player using your portal to the same block as you whether they are above or below you. You cannot teleport players if you are above y=80.
Starting items: Compass and portal
Extra info: This is not my idea I've seen this kit on other servers and it is really helpful for tracking people who are mining or camping in towers. Once endermaged up both of you get a few seconds of invincibility to prepare for the fight :)

Kit name: Lumberjack
Kit info: Destroy one block of wood and the entire tree will collapse, breaking all the wood.
Starting items: Compass and woodaxe

Kit name: (Undecided)
Kit info: Every time you kill a mob who drops meat, you are guaranteed to get one cooked piece of food and a chance of a second piece from that mob.
Starting items: Compass
Extra info: This kit could possibly be a buff to the cook kit, instead of starting with 5 cooked steak you get this ability.

Kit name: Rogue
Kit info: Eat an apple and you gain 15 seconds of invisibility.
Starting items: Compass and 2 apples
^ Added to the server :D

Kit name: Hornet
Kit info: There is a 20% chance of giving your enemy the nausea potion effect for 5 seconds every time you hit them.
Starting items: Compass
Extra info: HornetJumper came up with the name :P 

Kit name: Prowler
Kit info: Eat a golden carrot and get the night vision effect for 1 minute and 30 seconds.
Starting items: Compass and 3 golden carrots

Kit name: Golem
Kit info: Always have resistance 1 and the slowness 1 potion effects.
Starting items: Compass
Extra info: Take less damage with resistance 1 but move slower with slowness 1.

Kit name: (Undecided)
Kit info: After killing a player you get a positive potion effect that lasts for 15 seconds.
Starting items: Compass
Extra info: Positive potion effects include speed, haste, strength, jump boost, regeneration, resistance, or invisibility.

Kit name: Detective
Kit info: When right-clicking with your compass, you are shown the exact amount of blocks the nearest player is away from you.
Starting items: Compass 
Extra info: Very helpful kit for tracking and hunting people down, also great for when mining you can right-click your compass every now and then to see if anybody is nearby. Looking for better name please suggest one :)

Kit name: Ares or Warrior
Kit info: After getting a certain amount of kills you are rewarded with a new sword.  
3 kills = stone sword 5 kills = iron sword, and 10 kills = diamond sword
Starting items: Compass and a yellow leather helmet
Extra info: After reaching 3 kills a stone sword is placed in your inventory, 5 an iron one is put in, and 10 is a diamond sword put in your inventory. This kit is designed to reward players that enjoy tracking and killing. The reason why iron sword is only two kills after the stone sword is because it isn't that hard to craft one during invincibility. Inspired by the Greek god of war, Ares. 

Kit name: Fisherman
Kit info: Start out with a boat and a fishing rod
Starting items: A compass, a boat and a fishing rod.
Extra info: Good for maps with lots of water, also good for getting food.

Kit name: Flare or Beacon
Kit info: Shoot off fireworks to regroup with your team or lure in unsuspecting enemies to an ambush. 
Starting items: A compass, 5 firework rockets with the effects flight duration 3, large ball, lime colour, and trail.
Extra info: Great kit to find your teammates, or attract and ambush enemies.

Kit name: Omnivore
Kit info: You can eat cactus and cocoa beans.
Starting items: A compass
Extra info: Eating a cocoa bean or a piece of cactus heals half a heart. This kit would be great for desert maps and jungle maps where it is hard to find food.

I love coming up with kit ideas, I will try and update this post with new kit ideas every few days or so :)
Feel like a kit needs a new name or is too overpowered/underpowered please post your suggestions :)

Thoughts?

Thanks guys :D
