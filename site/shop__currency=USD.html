Welcome




What is the shop?

The shop is a place for users to improve their overall experience on MC Zone. Here you can purchase account upgrades, various pets, and game kits. Find an option that is right for you below. When purchasing items from the shop, please ensure you type in the correct username and have full permission to use the money you are spending. If a chargeback occurs, you will be banned from MC Zone and all other servers using Buycraft.


What is the money used for? 

We can guarantee all payments to MC Zone will go towards either server costs or to the developers of MC Zone.


Are there coupon codes?

Throughout the year we will provide coupon codes on our website and in game, but primarily on our Twitter and Facebook pages. Make sure to follow and like them while you are visiting!


Questions?

If you have any questions regarding the MC Zone shop, feel free to contact the us at info@mczone.co.

Note: It can take up to 25 minutes for a donation to process. Please do not send an email regarding issues with payments until after 25 minutes. Thanks!
