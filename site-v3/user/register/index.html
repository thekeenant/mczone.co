Register Create a MC Zone account

Registering for Minecraft Zone is easy and quick! Simply login to one of our servers and type the command, "/register [password]", where [password] is the password you will use when logging into the website. Once you have created a password, you can click the account button on the top right and login using your Minecraft username and MC Zone password.
